SBI Small Cap Fund

The SBI Small Cap Fund primarily invests in equity and equity-related instruments of small-cap companies.

Risks in Small Cap Mutual Funds

Small-cap funds carry a higher degree of risk compared to large-cap and mid-cap funds. The key risks include:

  • High Volatility: The stock prices of small-cap companies can fluctuate wildly based on market sentiment.
  • Liquidity Risk: Small-cap stocks often have lower trading volumes, making it harder for the fund manager to buy or sell large quantities without impacting the price.
  • Business Risk: Smaller companies are more vulnerable to economic downturns and have a higher risk of business failure compared to established large-cap companies.

Exit Load

For the SBI Small Cap Fund, an exit load of 1.00% is applicable if the units are redeemed or switched out within 1 year from the date of allotment. Nil exit load applies for redemption after 1 year.
