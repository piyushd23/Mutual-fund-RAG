Capital Gains Statement

A Capital Gains Statement provides a summary of the profits or losses incurred from the redemption or sale of mutual fund units during a specific financial year. It is essential for filing your Income Tax Returns (ITR).

How to Download Capital Gains Statement

Investors can download their Capital Gains statement using any of the following methods:

  1. SBI MF Website: Visit the official sbimf.com portal, log in to your dashboard, navigate to the 'Statements' section, and select 'Capital Gains Statement'.
  2. MFCentral: Since MFCentral is the unified hub, you can log in to mfcentral.com using your PAN, go to 'Service Requests', and download a consolidated Capital Gains Statement across all RTAs (CAMS and KFintech).
  3. CAMS / KFintech Mailback Services: You can request the statement via email from the CAMS or KFintech mailback services by entering your registered email ID and PAN.
